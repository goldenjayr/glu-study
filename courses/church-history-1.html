The Apostolic Church, Pentecost to 100 AD

The Apostolic Church from Pentecost to AD 100 — Jerusalem Council welcoming Gentiles, Pentecost as Christianity’s foundation and new worship model (preaching, lifestyle, spiritual life), Peter’s Joel 2:28–32 sermon, and the early church’s missionary-pastoral prayer life.

Smyrna & Pergamos: The Persecuted and Worldly Church (100–800)

Smyrna the Persecuted Church (100–313) and Pergamos the Worldly Church (313–800) — John the last Apostle (~100), early learning and worship, Justin Martyr’s order of worship, Ambrose’s doctrinally pure hymns, desert refuge under persecution (to Diocletian), Jesus’ word to Pergamos (Balaam/Nicolaitans), Augustine (Confessions & City of God; not first to use Trinity), and Patrick of Ireland.

CompleteQuiz 100%
6

Thyatira: The Medieval Church (800–1517)

Thyatira the Medieval Church (800–1517) — Middle Ages from Rome’s fall (476); East/West split not immediately after the First Crusade; minimized Bible preaching (poor priest training); congregational singing not critically important (yet Francis emphasized it); Waldo, Wycliffe, Huss, and Savonarola as Reformation forerunners; by 1500 four of five original Christian stronghold cities under Islam.
