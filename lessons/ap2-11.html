Why Can’t We Hear The Actual Voice Of God?

Why God may be silent — and how He still speaks through Word, Son, nature, and standing orders.

CompleteQuiz 100%
At a glance

Summary

God may be silent for many reasons, but He always speaks through His Word and spoke most clearly through His Son. Silence can be a test; His last orders are standing orders. Claims of hearing God’s voice must be tested. We are not responsible for what God keeps secret, and He never promised to always explain what He is doing.

Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • Many reasons God might be silent — silence can be a test.
  • False that God never speaks through the natural world; True that He always speaks through His Word.
  • God spoke most clearly through His Son.
  • When silent: last orders are standing orders.
  • God can do whatever He wants because He is God.
  • A claim to hear God’s voice must be questioned — not automatic truth.
  • God never promised always to explain what He is doing; we are not responsible for His secrets.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

Ways God has spoken / how we hear today

God has spoken in many ways: through creation and the natural world; through Scripture, His breathed-out Word; through judgment and the Holy Spirit’s conviction; through miracles and signs; and at times through an audible voice. He has also guided people without words, through His Spirit, through circumstances or providence, and ultimately through Jesus Christ, the clearest and fullest revelation of God. The Bible and the life of Christ remain the reliable message we are to hear today.

Seven situations when God seems silent — and how to respond

The seven situations are: God is not ready to speak; He is testing me; He has already talked to me; He wants me to make an important decision; He knows I will not listen; I have forgotten what He said; or something is hindering my ability to listen. I should not panic or invent a new message. I should treat His last orders as standing orders, remember clearly what He has already said, stay in prayer and Scripture, examine what may be blocking my hearing, and wait faithfully. Like David, I should hope in God and learn to listen through the sounds of silence.

Why audible voice is not God’s normal way today (Towns)

Dr. Towns says God can speak audibly because He is God, but He has chosen not to make that His normal way of speaking today. During the age of grace, God does not show partiality: Christ has made access to God available to all, so a private audible message to one person would raise the question of why not everyone. God has already spoken fully through His Son, Jesus, and has recorded everything necessary in the Bible. The written Word is consistent, reliable, and available to every believer; the Holy Spirit helps us understand it, and Christians should study it together and test claims against Scripture. People who claim an audible message may be mistaken, self-deceived, mentally ill, lying, or hearing something demonic, so the spirits must be tested. Even an audible voice would not guarantee faithfulness, guidance, character growth, or doctrinal truth. God is not silent—He speaks through His Word, prayer, circumstances, and other believers.
