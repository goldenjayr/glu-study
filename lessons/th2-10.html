Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • The Spirit restrains sin — the Church is one instrument — and prevents the complete corruption of the world.
  • Baptism of the Spirit is a one-time work at conversion that places the believer into the body of Christ (1 Cor 12:13). It is not a second blessing and not water baptism.
  • Indwelling is permanent from conversion. Filling is repeated control; believers are commanded to be filled. Filling is not getting more of God; it is yielding more of yourself.
  • The seal is the presence of God in the believer. Illumination is a ministry of subtraction: the Spirit removes spiritual blindness.
  • Sign gifts do not vastly outnumber serving gifts. The unpardonable sin is the final rejection of Christ through unbelief.
  • Character is habitually knowing and doing the right thing in the right way. Fullness does not mean immerse. Regeneration was not emphasized by Luther (Moodle: False).

Essay themes

If you preach or review

Explain what is meant by the term, "the baptism of the Holy Spirit."

One-time work at conversion: the Spirit places the person who receives Christ into the body of Christ (1 Cor 12:13). Not a second blessing to seek, and not water baptism. Historically at Pentecost for the church; personally at new birth.

Explain the Filling of the Spirit. How does it differ from the Indwelling of the Spirit?

Filling is repeated control and power for living and service (Eph 5:18) as we yield, confess, and obey. Indwelling is the Spirit’s permanent presence from conversion (1 Cor 6:19; Rom 8:9). Every believer is indwelt; not every believer is always filled. Filling is not getting more of God; it is letting Him have more of you.

List and explain the serving gifts of the Holy Spirit.

Prophecy — proclaim God’s message. Teaching — explain and apply truth. Exhortation — encourage and challenge. Shepherding — care, guide, protect. Word of knowledge — communicate spiritual knowledge. Ministering — practical service. Giving — generous sharing. Ruling — diligent leadership. Evangelist — proclaim the gospel.
