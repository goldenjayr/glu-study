Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • Demons are fallen angels: personal, nonmaterial, intelligent, immoral, powerful but limited.
  • They oppose God through temptation, deception, false teaching, affliction, and spiritual warfare.
  • Demon worship includes divination, necromancy, magic (white and black), sorcery, and witchcraft — all drawing power apart from God.
  • God can use even a demon to accomplish His will. Some demons are confined until the Tribulation.
  • Deliverance depends on God’s power, faith, Scripture, and a spiritually mature counselor — not the counselor’s personal power.
  • The Hallucination Theory is not “a vivid symbol of the prevalence of evil” — that is the Mystical Theory. Review that miss.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

Please explain Dr. Towns' nine-point description of demons.

1) Spirit nature — nonmaterial, can indwell, sometimes appear. 2) Unity — organized under Satan. 3) Fear of judgment — they know Christ’s authority. 4) Essence — immortal, powerful, but intellect and morals corrupted. 5) Power — great strength, limited by God. 6) Intellectual — accumulated knowledge, false doctrine, no saving faith. 7) Personality — desire, emotion, will (the swine). 8) Moral — evil, reprobate, unclean, no repentance. 9) Number — about one-third of the angels fell with Satan.

List and describe the five characteristics of demon worship.

1) Divination — illegitimate attempt to know God’s will or the future (e.g. examining an animal’s liver). 2) Necromancy — interrogating the dead; an abomination. 3) Magic — white and black both from demon power; not parlor tricks. 4) Sorcery — calling on demons for supernatural effects. 5) Witchcraft — magic and sorcery to do Satan’s will (Deut 18:10). All seek power apart from God.

List and explain the three qualifications of an exorcist.

1) Spiritual — born of the Spirit, obedient, Spirit-controlled. 2) Scriptural — knows what the Bible teaches about God, Satan, and demons; uses God’s armor; convinced of Christ’s victory. 3) Special — medical/psychiatric training when possible; a mature Christian with experience is preferred. God delivers. The counselor has no personal power; he points the person to Christ, Scripture, faith, confession, and a willing follow.
