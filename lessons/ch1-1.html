Lesson 1

What is history (makers, evidence, providence) and what is worship from an Old Testament perspective (face-to-face, praise, sacrifices).

CompleteQuiz 100%
At a glance

Summary

Lesson 1 defines history as a careful account and interpretation of past people, events, cultures, and forces — using evidence while recognizing perspective and God’s providential work. History makers make history; the Jewish view of history is not the cycle view; events, dates, and places alone do not make history come alive. Worship begins with a face-to-face encounter with God as a whole-life relationship; Old Testament praise is the most significant form, and sacrifices from the beginning pointed toward Christ.

Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • History makers make history — people shaped by conviction and calling drive the story.
  • The Jewish view of history is not identical to the cycle view of history.
  • Events, dates, and places alone do not make history come alive.
  • History is a careful account and interpretation with evidence, perspective, and God’s providence.
  • Worship begins with a face-to-face encounter with God; it is a whole-life relationship.
  • Music style in worship has not remained constant through church history; organs were not originally only Reformed.
  • Wesley’s songs had at least as great an effect as his sermons.
  • Abel, Noah, Abraham, and Moses worshipped; OT praise is the most significant form; sacrifices from the beginning pointed toward Christ.
Key terms

People, places, and words that matter

History (makers / providence)

A careful account and interpretation of past people, events, cultures, and forces, using evidence while recognizing perspective and God’s providential work. History makers make history.

Jewish vs cycle view of history

The Jewish view of history is not identical to the cycle view; treating them as the same is false.

Worship (face-to-face)

Worship begins with a face-to-face encounter with God — a whole-life relationship expressed through reverence, obedience, sacrifice, prayer, praise, thanksgiving, and service.

Wesley’s songs

Wesley’s songs had at least as great an effect as his sermons.

OT praise

The most significant form of worship in the Old Testament was praise.

Sacrifices

Part of Old Testament worship from the very beginning; expressed repentance, faith, gratitude, surrender, fellowship, and atonement, and pointed toward Christ’s sacrifice.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

How would you answer ‘What is History?’

History is a careful account and interpretation of past people, events, cultures, and forces, using evidence while recognizing perspective and God’s providential work.

From an Old Testament perspective, what is worship?

Whole-life relationship with the living God, expressed through reverence, obedience, sacrifice, prayer, praise, thanksgiving, and service.

Explain the role of sacrifices in Old Testament worship.

Sacrifices expressed repentance, faith, gratitude, surrender, fellowship, and atonement; the various offerings taught the seriousness of sin and pointed toward Christ’s sacrifice.
