Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • Angel means messenger. They are created, incorporeal, holy, personal, deathless, unseen, and organized — powerful, but not omnipotent or omniscient.
  • Lucifer originally held a high place in heaven. Gabriel’s name does not mean “who is like The Lord?” — that is Michael.
  • Scripture is the primary source of correct information about Satan. Adam and Eve were not created with a sin nature.
  • Satan is not “incapable of doing good”; he can appear to do good by imitation. His threefold work is opposition, imitation, and destruction.
  • Believers are commanded to resist the devil. Overcome him by caution, separation, resistance, and watchfulness with prayer.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

Describe the ten distinct works of the angels.

1) Transportation — carry the believer at death. 2) Revelation — deliver messages (never replacing Scripture or the Spirit). 3) Protection — encamp around those who fear God. 4) Liberation — open prison doors. 5) Military assistance — God’s host, the legions. 6) Influence — outward circumstances (Spirit works within). 7) Ministry — serve heirs of salvation. 8) Worship and praise. 9) Judgment — execute God’s punishment. 10) Appearance — visible when God has a special purpose. Strong, yet humble and obedient.

List and explain the three-fold works of Satan.

1) Opposition — resists God and seeks to destroy the race God loves. 2) Imitation — counterfeits God’s work; can appear as an angel of light. 3) Destruction — steal, kill, destroy; deceive nations, blind unbelievers, tempt and accuse believers. Real schemes, still subject to God.

List and explain the four principles involved in believers overcoming Satan.

1) Caution — do not play with satanic power or treat evil as entertainment. 2) Separation — abstain from the appearance of evil; reject false teaching. 3) Resistance — submit to God first, then resist the devil in the faith. 4) Watchfulness and prayer — watch and pray; put on the whole armor of God. Depend on God, not on personal strength.
