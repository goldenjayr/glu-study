Lesson 7 · Course 65

Logical Proofs That Jesus Is God

Jesus’ claims, worship, messianic fulfillment, and the virgin birth — proofs of deity that do not make a claim true merely by being spoken.

CompleteQuiz 100%
At a glance

Summary

A claim to be God is not true merely because it is claimed. Jesus claimed to forgive sins; NT authors ascribe creation to Him; worship given to and accepted by Him demonstrates deity. He saw Himself as OT Messiah fulfillment; He fills the offices of prophet, priest, and king; the virgin birth demonstrates deity (Matthew calls Mary a virgin; Luke also identifies virgin birth). A Christophany is not defined as a post-incarnation bodily manifestation of Christ as God.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

Give nine reasons why we can believe in the Deity of Jesus Christ.

1. Jesus openly claimed deity, using the divine “I AM” language and saying, “I and my Father are one.”
2. The Old and New Testament Scriptures testify that Jesus is God—the Word was God, Isaiah called Him Mighty God, and Paul called Him great God and Savior.
3. The triune nature of God places the Son fully within the one divine life of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
4. Jesus’ heavenly origin and eternal preexistence show that He did not begin at Bethlehem; He came down from heaven.
5. Scripture gives Jesus the work of creation: all things were made by Him.
6. The New Testament ascribes divine attributes to Him, including sustaining creation, eternal life, and authority beyond ordinary human limits.
7. Jesus received and accepted worship, something appropriate for God alone.
8. Scripture gives Him divine titles such as Jehovah, Son of God, Son of man, Word, and Christ/Messiah.
9. His virgin birth and sinless life demonstrate a unique, holy divine-human identity, while His prophetic, priestly, and kingly offices fulfill God’s redemptive work.

Discuss Jesus’ own claims to be Deity.

Jesus did not leave His deity as an abstract claim; He spoke and acted in ways that put the claim plainly before His hearers. He used the divine “I AM” language, including “I am the way,” “I am the resurrection,” and “I am the door.” He said, “I and my Father are one,” and called God “my Father,” which His opponents understood as making Himself equal with God. He claimed authority to forgive sins, an authority they recognized belongs to God. He identified Himself with the Old Testament Lord and Messiah, claimed a unique oneness with the Father, and spoke of heavenly origin and authority. He also accepted worship rather than refusing it. Taken together—His words, His works, His holiness, His authority over sin, and His acceptance of worship—show that Jesus understood Himself to be the divine Son, not merely a teacher or prophet.

What five titles given to Christ in Scripture demonstrate His Deity?

Five titles that point to Christ’s deity are:
1. Jehovah—the Lord of the Old Testament, identifying Jesus with the divine Lord.
2. Son of God—the title expressing His divine nature and unique relationship with the Father.
3. Son of Man—the Daniel 7 title of the heavenly figure who receives everlasting dominion.
4. The Word—the eternal Word who was with God and was God, and through whom God revealed Himself.
5. Christ or Messiah—the Anointed One who fulfills the divine offices of Prophet, Priest, and King.
