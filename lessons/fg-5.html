Lesson 5 · Course 64

John

Jesus the Christ and Son of God -- believe, and have life.

At a glance

Summary

John presents Jesus as the Christ and Son of God so that readers believe and have life — signs, “I am” sayings, tabernacle/temple fulfillment, written from Ephesus for Jewish believers still in the synagogue outside Palestine.

Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • Emphases: Christ and Son of God, belief, life, eyewitness testimony, signs, “I am” sayings, fulfillment of God’s presence (tabernacle/temple).
  • Structure: introduction, public ministry, private ministry, conclusion. Public ministry divided by Passovers and feasts. Climax: Thomas — “my Lord and my God.” First sign: water to wine at Cana.
  • Author: John son of Zebedee (Jesus’ cousin, inner circle, Sons of Thunder, later Patmos); wrote from Ephesus; not named in the Gospel. Also 1–3 John and Revelation.
  • Audience: Jewish believers outside Palestine still in the synagogue. Purpose: confirm Jesus is both Christ and Son of God.
  • Four themes: Son of God, Christ, Life, Believing. “Believe” ~106× (34 in synoptics). Eternal life: joyful fellowship with God already beginning when we believe. “I am” 24× as claim of deity.
  • Fulfills tabernacle/temple by showing God’s presence in the world; sanctuary decorations modeled after Eden; foot washing symbolized His whole earthly ministry.
  • Feast of Dedication: 165 BC, Maccabees vs Antiochus Epiphanes, purifying the temple — today Hanukkah.
  • Quote: John is a pool in which babes wade and elephants swim. P52/Rylands ~100–150 AD. Eusebius quotes Irenaeus about John at Ephesus.

For the pulpit

Sermon hooks

  • Believe and live — John’s aim is faith that already tastes fellowship with God.
  • Preach the “I am” sayings as deity claims, not mere metaphors.
  • Babes and elephants: keep John simple enough for a child and deep enough for a lifetime.

  • Structure: intro → public → private → conclusion; climax = Thomas.
  • Themes: Son of God, Christ, Life, Believing (~106×).
  • I am ×24; eternal life already begun; foot washing = whole ministry.
  • Misses to review: signs wording; Eusebius quotes Irenaeus (not Clement).
