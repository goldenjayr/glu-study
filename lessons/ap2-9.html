Essay themes

If you preach or review

From the skeptic or universalist perspective, are the heathen really lost?

From the skeptic or universalist perspective, the heathen are not necessarily lost. Universalists believe all people will eventually go to heaven, including those who have never heard of Jesus, because God is loving and fair. They argue that such people may be judged by a different standard, given another opportunity after death, or accepted because of sincere worship. Some even minimize the severity of God’s judgment or deny hell.

List the ten questions every Christian must answer

1. Is there such a thing as absolute truth?
2. How can I know there is a God?
3. Where did the universe come from?
4. Is the Bible reliable?
5. Is Jesus the only way?
6. Are the claims of Jesus’ physical resurrection from the dead valid?
7. Are the heathen really lost?
8. Is hell a real place that lasts for eternity?
9. Must a person believe in Jesus or make Him Lord of his life to be saved?
10. How detailed is God’s wonderful plan for each individual life?

Scripture’s answer: are people outside the church lost?

Scripture answers that people outside the church are lost because salvation is found only in Jesus Christ. He is the way, the truth, and the life, and no one comes to the Father except through Him (John 14:6; Acts 4:12). All people are sinners, and those who have not heard still have an inborn knowledge of God and a conscience witnessing to right and wrong, yet they suppress and reject that knowledge. Sincerity, sacrifice, or religious practice cannot substitute for Christ; only those who believe in Him receive eternal life.
