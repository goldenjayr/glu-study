Essay themes

If you preach or review

Discuss the five Bible passages containing the Great Commission. How are they similar and how do they differ?

All send the disciples as witnesses to the world. They differ in setting, timing, number, and emphasis. John 20:21 (Upper Room, resurrection day, ten) — as the Father sent Me, I send you. Mark 16:15 (~a week later, eleven) — all the world, every creature. Matthew 28:19-20 (Galilee mountain) — make disciples, baptize, teach; authority and presence. Luke 24:46-48 (Jerusalem, day 40) — repentance and forgiveness to all nations, beginning at Jerusalem. Acts 1:8 (Olivet, day 40) — Spirit’s power; Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, uttermost earth.

What do we know about the relationship between the Great Commission and church planting? Explain.

They belong together. God’s primary method of evangelizing an area is planting a New Testament church. Making disciples includes winning, teaching, encouraging. Baptism brings the believer into the church. Teaching Christ’s commands is church work. A church reproduces after its kind — sending missionaries who plant churches that send more. The local church should reproduce in another neighborhood, town, or village. Only existing churches can be perfected and teach new believers.

Discuss the five commitments of a disciple.

1) Jesus Christ — follow, take up the cross, put Him first. 2) The church — fellowship and gathering. 3) The Word — abide in His word and obey it, not merely know it. 4) Love for other believers — the mark Jesus named. 5) Bearing much fruit — spiritual fruit and helping others follow Jesus. Discipleship is a continuing life of following, learning, loving, and serving.
