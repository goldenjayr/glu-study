Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • Synoptics vs John, Markan priority, kingdom in stages, genre as OT historical narrative with Greco-Roman biography features — Lesson 1.
  • Four portraits and how we follow Him — Lessons 1–5.
  • Matthew: Jewish Christians, Messiah King, kingdom of heaven, five sections plus intro and conclusion.
  • Mark: Rome, suffering, servant; earliest ending invites scribal additions.
  • Luke: salvation, Gentiles, Theophilus, Muratorian Fragment, Papyrus 75.
  • John: synagogue diaspora, Irenaeus, Ephesus, son of Zebedee / cousin, four themes.
