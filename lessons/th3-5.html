Lesson 5 · Course 57

What We Believe About The Doctrine Of The Church

The church as Christ’s body and stewardship — organism, assembly, and Great Commission.

CompleteQuiz 100%
At a glance

Summary

The church is an organism — the body of Christ — belonging to Christ, with embryonic truth in Scripture’s first reference. “Binding and loosing” are not uniformly understood by all believers. Dispensation means stewardship. “Church” has many popular meanings; the building analogy is not the most used; fold and flock are not identical. Theologically the church is an assembly of professing believers, and it ceases to be a church when it ceases the Great Commission.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

Explain the meaning of the word, ‘church.’

The word “church” comes from the Greek ekklesia, meaning an assembly of people who are called out. In the New Testament, believers are called out from the world and called together for God’s purpose. The word can refer to a local congregation, and in a broader sense to the universal body of all true believers in Christ. It is therefore not merely a building or a social organization, but a gathered people who belong to Jesus.

What is meant in Scripture by the term, ‘keys?’

The “keys” refer to the authority and responsibility Christ gives His servants to proclaim the gospel and open the way of salvation to people. Peter used these keys first among the Jews at Pentecost and then among the Gentiles in Cornelius’s house. They do not mean that a human leader controls heaven or forgives sins. The church announces God’s revealed truth; binding and loosing concern declaring what God forbids or permits according to Scripture, and proclaiming the result of the gospel.

Define the church’s six functions, and explain each.

The church has six main functions. First, it is an assembly of professing believers: Christians gather as a visible fellowship, while the church must also seek genuine faith in those who profess Christ. Second, it is the place where the unique presence of Jesus Christ dwells; He is the church’s life and light. Third, it lives under the authority of God’s Word, continuing in apostolic doctrine and accepting the discipline and correction of Scripture. Fourth, it is organized to carry out the Great Commission by preaching Christ, making disciples, baptizing, and teaching believers to obey Him. Fifth, it administers the two ordinances, baptism and the Lord’s Table; baptism pictures identification with Christ’s death, burial, and resurrection, while the Lord’s Table brings edification, fellowship, and self-examination. Sixth, it reflects spiritual gifts, with members and leaders serving one another and the mission of the church through the gifts God gives them.
