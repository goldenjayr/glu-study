Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • The Holy Spirit is a divine Person — intellect, emotion, and will — identified as God. He is “God in us.”
  • Extensive research on the Spirit is more modern; it was not a major field before the twentieth century.
  • The Council of Nicea (325) did not first issue the “proceedeth from the Father” decree (Moodle: False).
  • Emblems include the dove, seal, anointing oil, fire, rain, wind, rivers, dew, water, clothing, and earnest — each picturing a facet of His character or work.
  • Ministry words (Towns): convict, restraint, regeneration, baptism, indwelling, illumination, sealing, prayer, fullness, sanctification, fruit, gifts.
  • Before the incarnation His ministry was not merely “manifestation.” He participated in creation, and He convicts before conversion.

FalseSerious, extensive study of the Spirit is largely a modern (twentieth-century) development.

2. The Holy Spirit has intellect, emotion, and volition (will).

TrueHe knows, He loves, He wills — Person, not force.

3. In Scripture, the Holy Spirit is identified as God.

TrueScripture names Him God and ascribes divine works to Him.

4. “God in us” is the Holy Spirit.

TrueMoodle punctuation kept. The indwelling Spirit is God with us, in us.

5. The Council of Nicea in 325 AD first issued the decree that recognized the Holy Spirit ‘who proceedeth from the Father.’

FalseNicea 325 is not where that procession language first lands. Moodle: False.

6. One emblematic representative of the Spirit is the dove.

TrueThe dove pictures the purity of the Spirit’s character (baptism of Jesus).

TrueMiss / partialMiss — Jay-R chose False. Moodle: True. The Holy Anointing Oil pictures the Spirit’s blessing (holiness / His presence on a person).

FalseHe participated in creation. Moodle: False.

FalseMoodle: False. His pre-incarnation work was more than mere manifestation.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

Briefly describe the proofs that the Holy Spirit is a person.

He has intellect, emotion, and will (1 Cor 2:11; Rom 15:30; 1 Cor 12:11). He teaches, testifies, guides, speaks, enlightens, strives, commands, intercedes, sends, calls, comforts, and works. People can obey, lie to, resist, grieve, and blaspheme Him. Scripture uses masculine pronouns. Person, not force.

List the emblems describing the Holy Spirit and provide the brief meaning for each.

Dove — purity. Seal — security and proprietorship. Holy Anointing Oil — holiness. Act of anointing — His presence on a person. Oil — divine qualification. Fire — visible operations. Rain — fullness of supply. Atmosphere — spiritual worship. Wind — winnowing/searching. Rivers — abundant grace. Dew — refreshing and fruitfulness. Water — spiritual life. Clothing — equipment and strength. Earnest — sample of promised glory.

List and explain the 12 words that describe the Holy Spirit's ministry according to Dr. Towns.

1) Reprove/convict — sin, righteousness, judgment before conversion. 2) Restraint — holds back evil. 3) Regeneration — new life. 4) Baptism — places believers into the one body. 5) Indwelling — the body as temple. 6) Illumination — understanding truth. 7) Sealing — mark and keep. 8) Prayer — helps and intercedes. 9) Fullness — fill and control. 10) Sanctification — into Christ’s image. 11) Fruit — Christlike character. 12) Gifts — abilities for ministry.
