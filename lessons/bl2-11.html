Big ideas

What to teach and remember

  • Every Christian has at least one spiritual gift and should discover and use it. Lack of a gift is not an excuse to sit out.
  • A spiritual gift is a Holy Spirit-given ability for serving the body of Christ. Gifts are usually given embryonically and mature as they are exercised.
  • Discovery involves Scripture study, inventory, trial and error, Spirit-led service, and counsel from mature believers.
  • Gifts help communicate the gospel, meet needs, develop ministry, and build church unity. Success in ministry: calling, gifts, and platform.
  • Abuses include ignorance, blindness, imposing, gravitation, colonization, coveting, assimilation, intrusion, manipulation, imitation, immaturity, and obsession.
  • Five biblical words: Pneumatikon (spiritual character), Charismata (gracious bestowal), Diakonia (service), Energema (God’s activating power), Phanerosis (visible manifestation).

Essay themes

If you preach or review

What are spiritual gifts and how might a believer go about discovering what gift(s) she/he possesses?

Special abilities from the Holy Spirit for productive service in the body. Discover by studying Scripture, taking an inventory, serving in different areas (trial and error), asking mature believers for counsel, and listening to the Spirit where God gives fruit, desire, and usefulness. Use the gift — do not only name it.

Discuss the relationship between spiritual gifts and ministry.

Gifts are the channels of ministry: they enable believers to communicate the gospel and meet people at their point of need. Used well, they establish a basis for leadership. Ministry builds people; leadership builds the church. A wise pastor delegates ministry but retains leadership. Different gifts help the body work in unity.

Discuss the five biblical words used to identify spiritual gifts. What is unique about each?

Pneumatikon — spiritual in character; the Spirit is the source. Charismata — from charis (grace); freely given, not earned. Diakonia — ministry/service; an opportunity to serve people. Energema — operation, God’s energy activating the ministry, more than natural ability. Phanerosis — manifestation; an evident work of God made visible through the believer.
