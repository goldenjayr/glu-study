What to teach and remember

  • Leadership is influence — not making people do what you tell them. It requires direction, followers, credibility, communication, accountability, motivation, problem-solving, and decision-making.
  • If the followers are not with the leader on the journey, both are just taking a walk.
  • Spiritual gifts and temperaments shape leadership strengths and weaknesses. The primary gift of leadership is not “helps.”
  • Biblical models: Moses’ delegation, Joshua’s mentoring, Jesus’ servant leadership, Peter’s growth after failure, and Paul’s strategic planning and disciple-making.
  • Joseph planned long-range. Solomon was a transitional leader. Esther led through hostility and crisis.
  • People look up to leaders. Leaders look down to followers. Leadership implies both movement and direction.

Essay themes

If you preach or review

How might the four temperaments influence our leadership style?

Sanguine — friendly, lively, motivating; can be weak-willed and disorganized. Choleric — independent, active, decisive; can be bossy and lack compassion. Melancholy — analytical, gifted, sensitive, faithful; may be pessimistic, fearful, moody. Phlegmatic — calm, dependable, peacemaking; may stay a spectator and avoid leading. Know the strengths and weaknesses; do not mainly work from the weak side.

What can we learn about leadership from Moses and Joshua?

Moses: charismatic and clear about God’s call, and he must delegate — judges, a team for administration and conflict. Joshua: mentored by Moses, prepared others, moved from victory to victory in military and diplomatic work. Together: vision, delegation, mentoring, and learning in the situation God gives.

What can we learn about leadership from Jesus, Peter, and Paul?

Jesus — servant leadership and shepherd leadership: serve, mentor, give His life for the sheep; credibility is ministry to others. Peter — self-correcting: failed, learned, became a respected leader. Paul — strategic planning and disciple-making: planned the work and trained other leaders so the work would continue after him.
