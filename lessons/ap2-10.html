Essay themes

If you preach or review

According to Dr. Towns, what is prayer?

According to Dr. Towns, prayer is based on a relationship with God in which we simply talk with Him. It is communication and conversation with the Father, not a mechanical formula or a way of manipulating Him. Through Christ, a believer who has a relationship with God can bring requests, worship, and thanksgiving to Him.

With respect to prayer's effectiveness in bringing change in the natural world, what is the critics' (skeptics') argument, and how can we respond?

Skeptics argue that the universe is a closed mechanical system controlled by fixed scientific laws. If prayer could change events in the natural world, they say it would interrupt cause and effect and produce chaos, so miracles and answers to prayer must be myths or mistaken interpretations. We can respond that God is the Creator and the Source and Lawgiver of nature. He is not trapped inside the naturalist's box; He can work through providence, bring natural forces together, or answer by a higher law and perform a miracle for His divine purpose. This does not mean prayer controls God. We pray in submission to His will and according to His Word.

Why would we believe that prayer could override God's plans for the universe?

I would not say that prayer overrides God's plans as though God were surprised or forced to change His mind. God is omniscient and sovereign, and He already knows the requests we will bring. Rather, prayer is one of the means God uses within His plan, and He may include our prayers in the way He accomplishes His will. When He answers with providence or a miracle, He is not abandoning His purposes; He is carrying out a divine purpose through the prayer of His people. Therefore we pray with faith, but also with submission to God's will.
